## 🌐 About NursePath

*NursePath* is a comprehensive nursing education platform designed to support Filipino nurses at every stage of their journey — from first-year student to doctoral candidate and global practitioner. Built on Philippine standards (CHED) and aligned with international frameworks (ICN, WHO, AACN, NMC, NMBA, CNA).

### 🎯 Primary Audience
- 🇵🇭 Filipino Nursing Students (Year 1–4)
- 🎓 New Graduates & PNLE Candidates
- 🏥 Licensed Registered Nurses
- 📗 Master of Science in Nursing Students
- 📕 PhD / DScN / DNP Candidates
- 🌍 Global Nursing Professionals & Leaders

### 📋 Standards & Frameworks
> CHED CMO 15 s.2017 | CHED CMO 5 s.2024 (MSN) | ICN | WHO | AACN | NMC | NMBA | CNA | PQF Levels 6–8

## 🏗️ Platform Structure — 6 Integrated Zones

### 📘 Zone 1 — Undergraduate (Year 1–4 + PNLE)
- Full 4-year curriculum aligned with CHED CMO 15 s.2017
- Clinical hour tracker & competency checklists
- NANDA-I, NIC, NOC, ICD-11 references
- *PNLE Reviewer:* 5 subjects × 20%, 15,000+ items, mock exams, AI weakness analysis, daily drills, readiness score

### 🌍 Zone 2 — International Licensure
- *NCLEX-RN* (NCSBN 2024)
- *UK NMC + OSCE*
- *Australia AHPRA / NMBA*
- *Canada NCLEX / CNA*
- *New Zealand NCNZ*
- *Ireland NMBI*
- *Singapore SNB*
- Blueprint-aligned banks, document checklists, scope of practice comparisons

### 🏥 Zone 3 — Practitioner
- *10 Specialty Reviewers:* Critical Care, Pedia, OB, Psych, Oncology, Med-Surg, Community, ER, Geriatrics, Leadership
- Clinical Tools: Drug Guide, Lab Values, Care Plan Builder, Calculators, EBP Library, Emergency Protocols
- *CPD Tracking:* PRC + International Revalidation

### 🎓 Zone 4 — Graduate (Masteral + Doctoral)
> 📗 *Masteral Tab (Blue Theme)* — CHED CMO 5 s.2024 compliant
> MSN/MScN programs, entrance exam reviewer, thesis builder, research tools

> 📕 *Doctoral Tab (Purple Theme)* — PhD/DScN/DNP
> Program pathways, qualifying exams, dissertation companion, advanced research toolkit

### 📚 Zone 5 — Study Lounge
- AI Flashcard Generator • Smart Spaced Repetition (SM-2 Algorithm) • 500K+ pre-made decks
- AI Tutor • Deep Study Mode • Practice Tests • Syncable Notes

### 🟣 Zone 6 — Community & Global Network
- Role-based communities • Mentorship program • Global job board • Scholarships • Publication alerts
